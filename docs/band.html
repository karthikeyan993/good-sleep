Inside, bottom to top

Strap

  • Quick-release pins: press the little lever and the pin retracts, then let go and it clicks into the lug holes. Any 20 mm watch strap fits.
  • Hook-and-loop threads through its ring and sticks to itself, so you set the tension by the millimetre: snug enough that the window stays on skin, loose enough to sleep.
  • Measure your wrist with a paper strip and check the strap's wrist range. The upper arm needs a long armband instead.

Case

  • Two printed parts: the body (walls, lugs, USB-C slot, switch opening) and the back plate (window and light barrier), joined by small screws or a snap fit. That's a Phase 4 decision.
  • PETG or resin from a print service, with every edge that touches skin rounded.
  • The window sits flush or slightly proud, so the sensor presses gently into the skin.

Not shown

  • The wires between the boards.
  • The optional 16 MB flash board. A breakout adds roughly 3 mm of height; soldering the bare chip (5 × 6 mm) avoids that.
  • The final shape. This is a box built around the parts. A custom PCB in Phase 6 is how it gets thinner and smaller.
